# Features

WorkAdventure has a number of features that make it a great platform for online collaboration.

## Video chat

When you get close to another user, a video chat will automatically start. You can see and hear the other user, and they can see and hear you.

## Screen sharing

You can share your screen with other users in a video chat. To do this, click on the screen sharing icon in the video chat window.

## Chat

You can use the chat to send text messages to other users. The chat can be accessed by clicking on the chat icon in the action bar.

## Megaphone

If you have the appropriate rights, you can use the megaphone to send a message to all the users in the world. This is useful for making announcements.

## Customization

You can customize your avatar by choosing from a variety of different skins and accessories. You can also customize your office by adding furniture and other objects.
