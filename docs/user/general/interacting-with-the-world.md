# Interacting with the world

You can interact with the world in WorkAdventure by moving your avatar close to objects or other users.

## Interacting with objects

When you move your avatar close to an object that you can interact with, the object will be highlighted. A message may also appear at the bottom of the screen.

To interact with the object, you can press the **Space** bar on your keyboard.

Interacting with an object can have different effects, such as:

- Opening a website
- Watching a video
- Opening a meeting room
- and much more!

## Interacting with other users

When you move your avatar close to another user, a video chat will automatically start. You will be able to see and hear the other user, and they will be able to see and hear you.

You can also use the chat to send text messages to other users.
