# Moving your avatar

You can move your avatar in WorkAdventure using the arrow keys on your keyboard.

- **Up arrow**: Move your avatar up
- **Down arrow**: Move your avatar down
- **Left arrow**: Move your avatar left
- **Right arrow**: Move your avatar right

You can also use the **WASD** keys to move your avatar.

On mobile devices, you can move your avatar by using the virtual joystick that appears on the screen.
